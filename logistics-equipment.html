How It Works: From request to delivery

  1. Submit your request.
  2. We source it: we coordinate sourcing once we receive your written request.
  3. Receive a written quote before you commit and proof approval for customized items.
  4. Approve, prepay, and we schedule your delivery.

Every order is sourced individually and quoted in writing. Local delivery is scheduled for Monday or Tuesday when confirmed; carrier shipping is quoted where appropriate.

Service clarity

Equipment is sourced and the purchase is coordinated by Legacy Logistics. We are not a dealer, and we do not service or repair equipment.

Equipment Placement

When equipment makes more sense placed than purchased outright — copiers and office machines are the common case — we coordinate placement through equipment dealers: the right machine for your volume, terms in writing before you sign, and service stays with the dealer who placed it. You deal with one coordinator; the dealer handles installation and service.
