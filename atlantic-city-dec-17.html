Traveling with us — comfort & accessibility

Most of the coaches on our trips are late-model — within the last four years — with TVs and other comfort amenities on board. Our full-size motorcoaches have an onboard restroom; mini coaches do not.

We have room on board for a limited number of travelers using walkers or wheelchairs. If you need a special accommodation — a walker, a wheelchair, or other equipment — please tell us when you book, so we can reserve the space and make sure we never overbook the accessible seating available on that trip.

Tour Protection — a cancellation waiver

Tour Protection is a house cancellation waiver, not insurance, and it covers the transport fare only. It costs $10.68 on an $89 seat and $9.48 on a $79 seat.

With Tour Protection, cancel for any reason up to 48 hours before departure for a full transport-fare refund — cash or Future Experience Credit plus 10%, your choice. The protection fee itself is non-refundable, except if Legacy Wise cancels the trip. How a protected cancellation pays out: if this trip has a waitlist, we will purchase your seat back at face value — the full amount you paid for the seat. If there is no waitlist, Tour Protection reimburses the bus fare only; any tickets or admissions included in the trip price are not reimbursed and remain yours to keep, transfer, or resell. You may instead transfer your registration to another person of your choosing, with our approval — transfers are free of charge.

Standard cancellation without Tour Protection: 30 or more days before departure, a full refund less $25 per person; 8–29 days before departure, 50% refund; inside 7 days, no cash refund, with 50% to Future Experience Credit. Seats are transferable free of charge before departure, to a person of your choosing, with our approval.

FREE Christmas add-on: Magic of Lights on the way home

On the way home, the coach drives through Magic of Lights at the PNC Bank Arts Center, 116 Garden State Parkway, Holmdel — directly on the Atlantic City to Staten Island return route. It is included in the $89 / $79 fare at no extra charge to you.

Everyone stays in the coach for the drive-through, which runs about 20–30 minutes, with 30–45 minutes budgeted in total including the entry queue. Magic of Lights season runs November 20, 2026 through January 3, 2027.

$10 Future Experience Credit for completing the trip

Each paid traveler who books and completes the December 17 trip receives a $10 Future Experience Credit — coded to you, one voucher per traveler per trip — issued by email within 3 business days after the trip.

Terms: valid 12 months toward a future Legacy Wise Experience; one per person per future booking; no cash value; non-transferable; not combinable with another promo credit on the same future booking. No credit is issued if the trip does not run.

Cancellation and if the trip does not run

See the Tour Protection and standard cancellation terms above. If the trip does not run, it is your choice: a full refund of everything paid, including the protection fee, or moving your full payment to another Legacy Wise Experience. You are never moved to a credit by default.
