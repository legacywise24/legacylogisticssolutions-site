Booked for you, start to finish

Legacy Travel Planning is the travel agency service of Legacy Logistics Solutions. Tell us where you are going and we handle the booking — airline tickets, hotels, and ground transportation, arranged together as one trip with one complete price. No juggling airline and hotel websites, no separate bookings to keep track of. Business travel, family trips, group travel, and travel built around an event — one request covers it.

How it works

  1. Tell us the trip — dates, travelers, and where you are going. Use the request button below or call (929) 244-0744.
  2. We source the options and quote one complete price for the trip — flights, hotel, and ground transportation together.
  3. You approve the price, we book it, and we stay on the trip if plans change.

What we book

  • Airline tickets — domestic and international
  • Hotels and lodging
  • Complete itineraries — flights, hotel, and ground transportation arranged together
  • Group and event travel — paired with our motorcoach and ground transportation services
  • Cruise Coordination — cruises researched, planned and booked for you through our agency partners, on the same request-and-confirm model as the rest of the page — one complete price before you commit, with no online checkout.

Good to know

  • Legacy Logistics Solutions is a travel booking and coordination service — we are not an airline or a hotel, and bookings are placed through our travel agency partners.
  • Travel booking is request-and-confirm: there is no online checkout on this page. Every trip is quoted and confirmed with you before anything is booked.
  • Ground transportation in the New York tri-state area can be booked directly online through our transportation page; larger vehicles and groups are quoted through our intake form.
