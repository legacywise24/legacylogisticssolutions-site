Shipping & Parcel Invoice Audit

If you ship with the major parcel carriers, your invoices almost certainly contain late-delivery refunds you are owed and billing errors — most businesses never claim them. We audit your parcel invoices, find the refunds, and file for them. Our fee is a share of the refunds actually recovered and credited to your carrier account — nothing recovered, nothing owed. Because carrier refund windows are short, audits run on a recurring cycle.
