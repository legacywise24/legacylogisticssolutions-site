How It Works: From request to walkthrough

  1. Request — tell us the rooms, headcount, and timeline. A floor plan or photos help.
  2. Written quote — one project quote covering furniture, freight, and delivery/installation coordination, with specifications, finishes, and lead time stated.
  3. Approve & prepay — nothing is ordered until the client approves the quote and dimensions in writing.
  4. Delivery & setup — delivery and installation are coordinated, with a walkthrough with the client.

Notes

Legacy Logistics is a sourcing and procurement firm, not a furniture dealer or showroom. Installation is performed by qualified providers. Manufacturer warranties apply to all furniture; we coordinate any claim. Special-order furniture is non-returnable except for defects — terms are stated on your quote before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

How long will my furniture take?

Lead times are quoted per project — quick-ship typically 2-3 weeks, specified product 6-10 weeks. Your written quote states the lead time for your items.

How does pricing work?

Every price is quoted per written request. There are no public prices, and we confirm what is included in your written quote before you approve and prepay.

What about warranties?

Manufacturer warranties apply to all furniture. Legacy Logistics coordinates any warranty claim.

Who is this for?

Offices, professional suites, schools, nonprofits, and clubs that need furniture sourced and coordinated as a single project.
