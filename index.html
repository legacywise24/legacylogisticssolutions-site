How it works

  1. 1. Submit your requestTell us what you need, including quantities and any custom details.
  2. 2. We source itLegacy Logistics sources each request individually, at the lowest net cost it can obtain.
  3. 3. You receive a written quoteConfirm current pricing, terms, and availability before you commit.
  4. 4. Approve and prepayProcurement, vendor coordination, and local delivery are scheduled when your order is confirmed.

All orders are prepaid before procurement or production. Customization, design, embroidery and printing are quoted separately when applicable, and customized production begins only after payment and proof approval. Local delivery is scheduled for Monday or Tuesday when confirmed; carrier shipping is quoted where appropriate.

Cooperative purchasing: eligible organizations — nonprofits, schools, churches, and public agencies — can use pre-competed contracts (for example, through programs like OMNIA Partners) instead of running their own bid. We set up and manage the sourcing; eligibility is confirmed for your organization before anything is promised.
