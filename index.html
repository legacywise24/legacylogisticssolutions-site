How It Works: From request to delivery

  1. Submit your request. Tell us what you need, including quantities and any custom details.
  2. We source it for your organization. Legacy Logistics sources each request individually, at the lowest net cost it can obtain.
  3. You receive a written quote. Confirm current pricing, terms, and availability before you commit.
  4. Approve, prepay, and we’ll handle the rest: Procurement, vendor coordination, and local delivery are scheduled when your order is confirmed.

Every order is sourced individually. Request pricing for current availability and a written quote. All orders are prepaid before procurement or production. Customization, design, embroidery and printing are quoted separately when applicable. Customized production begins only after payment and proof approval. Local delivery is scheduled for Monday or Tuesday when confirmed; carrier shipping is quoted where appropriate.

Cooperative Purchasing

Cooperative purchasing lets eligible organizations use pre-competed contracts — for example, through programs like OMNIA Partners — instead of running their own bid. Legacy Logistics sets up and manages the sourcing for you.

We set up your cooperative sourcing; eligibility is confirmed for your organization before anything is promised.

Eligible organizations can include nonprofits, schools, churches, and public agencies. Ask us whether cooperative purchasing fits your next request.
