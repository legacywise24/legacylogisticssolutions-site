How It Works: From request to delivery

  1. Request — tell us what you need, the quantity, and your timeline.
  2. Written quote — we source it and send a written project quote with the specifications, lead time, and breakdown of what is included: products, freight, and delivery/installation coordination.
  3. Approve & prepay — nothing is ordered until you have approved the quote and specifications in writing.
  4. Delivery & setup — delivery/installation coordinated, with a walkthrough if needed.
