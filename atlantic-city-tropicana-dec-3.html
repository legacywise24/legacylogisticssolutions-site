How booking works

  1. Book and pay online — book your bus seat and pay by card at checkout. The full amount is due before the trip.
  2. Your seat is reserved when payment is received. Stripe emails your payment receipt, and we email your trip details and exact pickup times.
  3. If this trip doesn't run: the choice is yours — (1) a full refund of the bus fare, or (2) a credit toward another Legacy Wise experience.

Tour Protection — optional

Plans change. Add Tour Protection when you book — $9.48 per traveler on the $79 fare.

Standard cancellation terms (without Tour Protection): 30+ days before departure, a full refund of the bus fare, less a $10 per-person processing fee. Within 30 days, the fare is non-refundable, and no future credit is issued. If you booked with a card, the refund goes back to that card.
