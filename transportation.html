Who we are

Legacy Logistics Solutions, Inc. is an independent, nonexclusive transportation concierge, procurement and logistics coordination company. LLS helps schools, organizations, businesses, event planners and private groups source, arrange and coordinate transportation services through qualified independent transportation providers throughout the United States. LLS is not exclusively affiliated with or obligated to use any particular transportation provider, bus company or charter brokerage. LLS independently identifies transportation needs, sources competitive provider quotations, evaluates service options, negotiates arrangements and coordinates transportation logistics on behalf of customers.

How Our Transportation Concierge Service Works

  1. Tell Us What You Need — submit requirements: dates, locations, passenger count, itinerary, special considerations.
  2. We Source Transportation Providers — LLS identifies and contacts qualified independent providers.
  3. We Evaluate Transportation Options — proposals, service requirements, equipment, pricing.
  4. We Coordinate Your Booking — once you approve the proposal and terms, LLS coordinates with the selected provider.
