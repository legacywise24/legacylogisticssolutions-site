One coordinator for the whole job

Some jobs are not a single ride or a single delivery — they are a move, a relocation, or a project with ten moving parts. Legacy Logistics Solutions plans and coordinates the whole job: the vendors, the vehicles, the schedule, and the site work, under one coordinator and one complete price.

What we handle

  • Vendor Coordination — We line up, schedule, and manage the vendors for your job, so you deal with one coordinator instead of a dozen contacts.
  • Relocation Services — We plan and coordinate relocations, from the first walkthrough to the final delivery, on one schedule.
  • Move Management — We manage the whole move — the planning, the vendors, the vehicles, and the timing — so nothing is left to chance.
  • College Move-In Services — We coordinate move-in for students, including delivery, setup, and scheduling around move-in day.
  • College Move-Out Services — We coordinate move-out, including pickup, packing help, and storage or shipping arrangements.
  • Property Support — We coordinate support for a property — deliveries, setup, and site work — handled in one request.
  • Site Coordination — We coordinate the people, vehicles, and work on site, so the job runs in the right order and on time.
  • Project Logistics — We plan and coordinate the logistics of a project — the schedule, the vendors, and the deliveries — under one complete price.

Good to know

Legacy Logistics Solutions is a coordination and sourcing service — work is performed by licensed, insured third-party vendors and carriers we source and coordinate. Every job is quoted individually after a short consultation; there are no fixed prices on this page.
