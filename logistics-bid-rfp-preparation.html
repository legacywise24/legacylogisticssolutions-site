Bid & RFP Preparation

Flat-fee preparation help for small businesses that bid government and institutional work under their own name — registrations, finding live bids that fit, preparing and assembling the bid package, and follow-through after submission. We prepare the package; you are always the bidder of record.

How it works

  1. Tell us the bid, or the kind of work you want to chase. We confirm it is a fit and that the deadline leaves enough lead time to prepare properly.
  2. Flat fee, quoted in writing. One flat fee for the preparation work, agreed before anything starts — never a percentage of the contract and never a success fee.
  3. We prepare the package with you. Registrations, forms, documents and assembly — built from your information, your pricing and your certifications.
  4. You review, sign and submit. The bid goes in under your company's name, on your decision. You are always the bidder of record, and we support follow-through questions after submission.

The model, stated plainly

The fee is flat and quoted before any work starts. We prepare — you are always the bidder of record. We never charge success fees on government bids and never guarantee an award.

What this service is — and is not

This is a preparation service for businesses bidding under their own name. It is separate from Legacy Logistics Solutions' own contract and transportation work, where Legacy Logistics itself is the bidder.

We are not the bidder, not a joint venturer, and not your agent on the bid. We do not set your prices, make your certifications, or decide what you bid. Our work is the preparation: the registrations, the search, the paperwork and the assembly, done with you and checked against the solicitation's requirements.

What we do

  • Registrations for federal, state, and city vendor portals
  • Finding live bids that fit, including cooperative contracts
  • Preparing and assembling the bid package
  • Follow-through after submission

Lead time

Bids are taken on only with enough lead time to prepare properly.

No guarantee — and the limits of our role

Legacy Logistics Solutions, Inc. provides bid preparation support only. We do not guarantee that any bid will win, that any contract will be awarded, or that any vendor registration will be approved. Awards are decided solely by the purchasing agency, and no preparation service can change that.

You are solely responsible for the accuracy and completeness of everything submitted under your name — including your pricing, qualifications, certifications and representations — and for submitting by the agency's deadline. We prepare from the information you provide; we are not liable for errors in that information, for agency or portal errors or changes, or for the outcome of any procurement, including lost business or lost profits.

Our flat fee pays for preparation work performed, whether or not you win. It is never contingent on an award, and we never charge success fees on government bids.
