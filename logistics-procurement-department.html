How it works

  1. Consultation to map your buying. We start with a consultation to map your categories and volume.

  3. We run your requests. We run requests through the same Request Pricing process, on your approvals.
  4. Monthly summary. Each month you receive a summary of what was sourced and saved.

Three service levels

Three service levels exist based on how many active requests you run each month. The right level is set in consultation, so the service fits the volume of buying you actually do.
