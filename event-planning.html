What Event Planning does

Legacy Logistics Solutions, Inc. is an independent event planning, procurement and logistics coordination company. Our Event Planning division plans events end to end for clients — a client requests a desired event or experience, or Legacy builds a unique one — and oversees the live event as designated planner and host. We build the itinerary, prepare promotional material, send invitations, manage RSVPs and registrations, run check-ins, and procure the vendors and supplies the event needs. We are not a carrier and we do not own or operate vehicles; transportation, when your event needs it, is performed by qualified independent providers.

How Our Event Planning Service Works

  1. Tell Us About Your Event — submit requirements: dates, locations, guest count, itinerary, special considerations.
  2. We Source Providers & Vendors — LLS identifies and contacts qualified independent providers and vendors.
  3. We Evaluate Options — proposals, service requirements, arrangements, pricing.
  4. We Coordinate Your Event — once you approve the proposal and terms, LLS coordinates with the selected providers and vendors.
  5. We Help Manage the Details — itinerary, scheduling, communication, logistics.
