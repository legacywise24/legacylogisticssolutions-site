Three stops, one glowing day

Lancaster & Wheatland. Wheatland was home to James Buchanan — the only U.S. President from Pennsylvania — from 1848 to 1868. At Yuletide, the mansion is decorated as it would have been in his day, and a guided tour walks through how Christmas traditions — the tree, the turkey, the holiday table — took hold in America. Your Wheatland ticket is included in your fare. After the tour, the rest of the Lancaster block is yours: shops, sights, and lunch on your own.

New Hope gets a full hour — the storybook river town earns it. A full hour on the Delaware: a hot drink or empanada at the Ferry Market food hall, something sweet from Moo Hope or Nina's Waffles, and a walk halfway across the bridge to stand on the PA/NJ line for a photo. Snack, don't fill up — dinner is at Peddler's.

Peddler's Village after dark. A 42-acre village of winding brick paths and more than 60 shops, lit by roughly a million holiday lights as dusk falls. Wander the shops, watch the lights come up, and have dinner on your own — the Cock 'n Bull and the Village's other restaurants are steps apart — before we roll home.
