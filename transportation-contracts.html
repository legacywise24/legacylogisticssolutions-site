Yellow Bus Contract Handling

We're here whether a yellow bus can be secured or not.

If your program requires a yellow school bus, we work to secure one through licensed, insured carriers that operate them — and we handle the contract around it: requirements, solicitation, bid evaluation, award coordination, scheduling and service follow-through.

  • School-year, semester and summer-program yellow bus contracts
  • Supplemental and shortage coverage when your current provider can't cover a route or a date
  • Field trips, athletics, after-school and event transportation under one contract instead of trip-by-trip buying
  • Contract reprocurement when an existing agreement is expiring

Note: check your D.O.E. guidelines — some contracts allow a standard motorcoach to substitute when no yellow bus inventory is available.

A yellow bus is never guaranteed — availability depends on carrier inventory and your program's requirements, and we tell you that before you commit, not after.

Bus Contract Cost Review

What is your current bus contract actually costing you?

Send us the contract you have. We break it down line by line and price the same service competitively:

  • Cost per route, per hour, per mile and per student — and how each is calculated
  • Deadhead / empty-mile charges, daily and hourly minimums, monitor / attendant charges
  • Fuel surcharges and annual escalators — what triggers them and what they have added
  • Extra-trip, late-cancel and change rates outside the base contract
  • Renewal and auto-extension terms, and the date you have to act by

You get a written findings summary: what the contract costs, where it is above a competitive quote for the same service, and your options — renegotiate, re-bid, or leave it alone. We do not guarantee savings; we show you the numbers and handle whichever option you choose.

Full Contract Handling

  1. Requirements — routes, dates, passenger counts, vehicle and accessibility requirements, service standards.
  2. Solicitation — RFP/RFQ preparation and distribution to qualified independent providers.
  3. Evaluation — bids compared on total contract cost, equipment, coverage and terms — not headline price alone.
  4. Award & coordination — the selected provider contracted and scheduled; one point of contact for changes and problems.
  5. Reconciliation — invoices checked against contracted rates before you pay them.
  6. Renewal — expiration and notice dates calendared; reprocurement started before the deadline, not after it.

All services are subject to provider availability, applicable requirements and contract approval.
