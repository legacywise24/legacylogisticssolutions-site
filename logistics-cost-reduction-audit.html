How it works

  1. Send recent invoices. Send recent invoices for the categories you want reviewed.
  2. We benchmark and identify savings. We compare what you pay, line by line, against what the same things should cost.
  3. You approve which changes to make. Nothing changes without your approval.
  4. Savings are verified on your actual bills. Our share applies only to savings that show up on your bills.

The model, stated plainly

Our fee is a share of the savings we actually verify in your invoices. Savings are measured against your documented prior spending. If we find no savings, you pay nothing. There is no upfront fee to start.

Who it is for

This audit is for businesses spending steadily in any of those categories — office supplies, telecom, waste, shipping, energy, and recurring services — who have not had anyone renegotiate them.
